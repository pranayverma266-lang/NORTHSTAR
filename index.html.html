Why it's trustworthy

Every route shows its "why" — closure risk, slope, road class, weather, vehicle fit, confidence — with a deterministic rules-based fallback if the AI model is unavailable. Cached vector maps, SMS/USSD alert hooks, and store-and-forward sync keep core guidance working when the network drops, with clear stale-data warnings.
